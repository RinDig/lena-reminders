# Step: draft this week's reminders

## Who receives the result
Lena, the office manager.

## What comes in
`1-stuff/clients.csv` and Lena's three emails, using the names in `2-map.md`.

## What to do, in order
1. For each client, compare `sent_last_year` with `received_this_year`.
2. List what is still missing.
3. Skip clients with nothing missing.
4. Draft one short reminder per client in Lena's tone, naming exactly what is missing.
5. Put every draft in `drafts.md`, with a list at the top of who is missing what.

## What goes out, and where it lands
`drafts.md` in this folder, for Lena to read.

## If something is missing or does not add up
If `received_this_year` is blank, list the client under "Not checked yet" and do not draft. If a note says to ask Lena, list the client under "Ask Lena". Never guess.

## What this step must never do
Send anything. Lena reads every draft first.
