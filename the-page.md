# My page (example: Lena's firm)

A made-up example from Kishore's email of 7 October. Build the whole setup once on it, then do the same for your own work in `session-4-kit`.

## Who receives it
Lena, the office manager at a six-person accounting firm.

## The line
For Lena, I turn the client list and last year's document checklist into personalised reminder emails, so she stops spending Monday mornings chasing missing documents.

## The steps
1. Export the client list from the practice software (rule)
2. Look up which documents each client sent last year (lookup)
3. Work out what each client is still missing this year (lookup) **circled**
4. Draft a reminder email per client, listing exactly what is missing (rule)
5. Lena reads each draft and edits anything sensitive (judgment) **AI never does this**
6. Send, and mark the client as chased (rule)

## The circled step
Step 3. About 40 clients, checked by hand against last year's list. It takes Lena around three hours every Monday.

## The step AI must never do
Step 5, deciding what gets sent. These are the firm's clients and their money. A wrong reminder embarrasses the firm, so Lena reads every email first.

## Inputs
`1-stuff/clients.csv` (40 made-up clients) and three reminder emails Lena wrote by hand last year.

## Done, in Lena's words
"On Monday I want to open one list that tells me who's still missing what, with the emails ready to go."
